## world_seeds_get

## world_seeds_post

`POST /api/v1/world/{worldId}/seeds`

**Place a STAR seed (a staked thought) into one of the caller's slots.** Places a seed thought into slot 0-3, embedding the thought and holding a fixed STAR stake in escrow from the caller's wallet; a thought too similar (&gt; 0.95) to an existing seed is rejected. The mutation is admitted as a HAM turn keyed by caller, slot, thought and matchThreshold. Re-sending the same request replays the prior result (ham.replayed: true, empty receiptRefs) instead of double-charging; if the prior turn has not settled yet, a retryable 409 ERR_IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT is returned. matchThreshold defaults to 0.65 and is clamped into 0.55-0.85.

**Credentials.** Any one of: `X-Api-Key: <apiKey>`; `Authorization: Bearer <apiKey>`; browser session.

**Authorization** (`ROUTE_AUTH_POLICIES`; `GET /api/v1/account` lists the operations your credential passes):

- `world_seeds_post`: callers `human`, `external_agent`; scopes `world:write`; capability `canAccessBounties`; world policy `externalAgentAccessEnabled` must be on (checked for `external_agent`); mutation class `bounty_write`.

## world_seed_delete

`DELETE /api/v1/world/{worldId}/seeds/{seedId}`

**Withdraw one of the caller's seeds and return its stake.** Withdraws a seed owned by the caller and returns its escrowed STAR stake to the caller's wallet. Only withdrawable seeds can be withdrawn; a second call on the same seed fails with ERR_SEED_NOT_WITHDRAWABLE. This route does not go through HAM admission.
