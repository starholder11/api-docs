title: Create and Publish a Story
description: End-to-end guide to creating and publishing a story — one submit call, then extraction, media production, and registration — and the submission lifecycle behind it.
---

This guide walks through creating and publishing a story, and the submission lifecycle behind it. External creators write narratives, the platform analyzes them to extract world objects (entities, settings, relationships, scenes), generates a media plan listing the images that need to be produced, and then integrates everything into the knowledge graph. This pipeline exists so that every story added to the world is structurally consistent, richly linked, and accompanied by visual media.

**Creating, publishing, and starting the pipeline are one call:** `POST /api/v1/world/{worldId}/submit/narrative` (`narrative_submit`). There is no separate create or publish operation. The same recipe is published for agents as the `story.publish` workflow in [`/.well-known/ham`](/docs/discovery#4-workflows-whole-tasks), with every step's method, path, body rules, and the response that means "go on".

Every request below is a `world.submit` operation; the [Operation Reference](/docs/reference/world-submit) has the full schemas. All of them take your key in `X-Api-Key` (or `Authorization: Bearer`). They are shown against `starholder_main`; use a world whose publication policy admits you. An unknown world returns `404 ERR_PUBLISH_TARGET_NOT_FOUND`, and a world that denies you returns `403 ERR_WORLD_PUBLICATION_DENIED` with the reason in `error.details`.

The body-scoped `POST /api/v1/world/submit/narrative` (`narrative_submit_legacy`) is **deprecated**. It publishes into `body.worldId`, or into the bootstrap world when `worldId` is missing, which is rarely what you meant. Use the path-scoped route.

## Choose a Production Mode First

You decide how much of the story the platform produces **when you submit**, with `requestedWorkStrategy`. The pipeline starts on the lane that choice names, and you can switch lanes later until media production starts (see [Changing the Lane](#changing-the-lane-while-the-pipeline-runs)).

- **Lazy lane** (`platform_default`): the platform produces everything — extraction, graph, narration, the media bundle, 3D, video, and composed scenes — and finalizes the story. Nothing pauses for you, unless you also send `requestExtractionReview: true` to review the extraction first.
- **Attended lane** (`self_fulfill` or `post_bounty`): the platform produces the extraction, graph, narration, and a work manifest of the media the story needs, then waits. You produce that media yourself (`self_fulfill`) or fund a marketplace bounty for another creator (`post_bounty`). By default the pipeline also pauses after extraction so you can review and correct it; send `skipExtractionReview: true` to go straight to the manifest.

| You want | Send at submit | You do afterwards |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| The platform to produce everything | `requestedWorkStrategy: "platform_default"` (optional `creativeBrief`) | Nothing; poll the submission until `registered` (step 2) |
| To review the extraction, then have the platform produce everything | `"platform_default"` + `requestExtractionReview: true` | Step 3; approval starts platform production; watch `GET .../fulfillment` |
| To review the extraction, then make the media yourself | `requestedWorkStrategy: "self_fulfill"` | Steps 3, 6, 7, 8 |
| To make the media yourself, no review | `"self_fulfill"` + `skipExtractionReview: true` | Steps 6, 7, 8 |
| To review the extraction, then offer a bounty | `"post_bounty"` + `creativeBrief` + `requestedBountyBudgetStar` | Step 3; the bounty posts on approval; then review the fulfiller's media on the bounty (step 7) |
| To offer a bounty, no review | the same + `skipExtractionReview: true` | The bounty posts when extraction completes; review on the bounty (step 7) |
| To decide after reading the extraction | no `requestedWorkStrategy` | Step 5 with any strategy, then the matching steps |
| To decide after reviewing the extraction | no `requestedWorkStrategy` + `requestExtractionReview: true` | Step 3, step 5 before or after approval, then the matching steps |
| To start attended, then hand everything to the platform | any attended body | Step 5 with `platform_default` before media production starts |

`skipExtractionReview` and `requestExtractionReview` cannot both be `true`; that submission is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`.

### Changing the Lane While the Pipeline Runs

The lane is not fixed when the pipeline starts. Until media production starts, `POST .../manifest/strategy` (step 5) moves the running pipeline to another strategy, in either direction:

- **Attended to platform.** From an attended or undecided submission, choose `platform_default` during extraction, during extraction review, or once the work manifest is waiting on you. If the manifest is waiting, the platform starts producing all media at once. During review, the review stays open, and approving it starts platform production.
- **Platform to attended.** On the lazy lane, choose `self_fulfill` while extraction runs. With `requestExtractionReview`, you can choose `self_fulfill` or `post_bounty` any time until you approve. The pipeline then pauses for review (unless you skipped it or it is done) and waits on the work manifest. Without review, `platform_default` becomes final when extraction completes, and an unreviewed lazy-lane submission cannot switch to a bounty, because a bounty needs the extraction.
- **Undecided to any.** With no strategy at submit, choose any of the three after extraction.

Two rules bound the switch. Once media generation has started, or fulfillment is complete, the call returns `409 ERR_STRATEGY_SELECTION_CLOSED` and nothing changes. And a strategy that has been *selected* (`fulfillmentStrategy` is set, either by step 5 or when the platform applies `requestedWorkStrategy`) cannot be replaced: a different one returns `409 ERR_STRATEGY_ALREADY_SET`.

A switch is something you decide to do, not a step every submission takes. `workflow.nextActions` offers `choose_strategy` only while no strategy is decided, and a strategy you requested at submit is the decision: the workflow does not ask you to choose it again. To switch, call step 5 directly while the rules above allow it. The one exception is a requested `post_bounty` that could not be applied; then `choose_strategy` returns with all three strategies in `allowedStrategies`.

### The `workflow` object

Every submission response carries a `workflow` object: the submit `201`, the submission read, the brief, strategy, and approve responses, the manifest read's `202` pending answer, and the fulfillment read. It states your lane, where review stands, the one condition the submission is waiting on, and the exact calls that are valid now:

```json
{
  "lane": "attended",
  "strategy": "post_bounty",
  "strategySource": "requested",
  "extractionReview": "awaiting_creator",
  "waitingOn": "creator",
  "blockingReason": {
    "code": "extraction_review_required",
    "description": "Review the extraction. Approve it when _quality.viable is true; otherwise request corrections."
  },
  "nextActions": [
    { "action": "get_extraction", "method": "GET", "path": "/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/sub_…/extraction", "description": "…" },
    { "action": "submit_correction", "method": "POST", "path": "…/correction", "description": "…" },
    { "action": "approve_extraction", "method": "POST", "path": "…/extraction/approve", "description": "…" }
  ]
}
```

`workflow` is the contract: follow `nextActions` instead of re-deriving the rules below. `waitingOn` says who must act: `extraction`, `platform`, `creator` (you), `bounty_fulfillers`, or `none`. `blockingReason` says what that is. When the other surfaces look inconsistent, for example a partial extraction during `pipeline_running`, it is the one to trust:

| `blockingReason.code` | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| `extraction_running` | Extraction (or re-extraction after corrections) is still running | Poll; optional brief or strategy calls are listed |
| `extraction_review_required` | The pipeline paused for your review | Read the extraction; approve if `_quality.viable`, otherwise correct |
| `strategy_required` | Extraction is done and no strategy applies | Choose a strategy (step 5) |
| `media_upload_required` | `self_fulfill`: the manifest waits on your media | Steps 6 and 7 |
| `bounty_fulfillment_pending` | `post_bounty`: the bounty is posted and waits on a fulfiller | Poll `GET .../fulfillment` until `submitted`, then review on the bounty (step 7) |
| `media_production_running` | The platform is producing media | Poll |
| `media_generation_stalled` | Platform media generation stalled | Inspect `GET .../fulfillment` |
| `completion_required` | Your `self_fulfill` media is accepted | Complete (step 8) |
| `registration_running` | The platform is finalizing and registering the story | Poll |
| `rejected` | The submission was rejected | Read `rejection`; resubmit with a new `submissionId` if allowed |

`blockingReason` is `null` once the story is `registered`. While the submission waits on extraction or the platform, `nextActions` includes `poll_submission`: read the submission again in about ten seconds. An empty `nextActions` means the submission is done.

Only `workflow` tells you what the submission is waiting on. A response without `workflow` is not a blocking reason and does not change the wait. That includes every error, such as `422 ERR_MANIFEST_EMPTY` from the manifest read, and the manifest itself once it exists. Keep the last `workflow` you read, or read the submission again for a fresh one. A `202` pending is a wait, not an error, and it carries `workflow`.

## Overview

```
1. Create and publish         POST .../submit/narrative               → 201 accepted_for_extraction (+ production mode)
2. Poll the submission        GET  .../submit/{submissionId}          → status + workflow (until registered)
3. Read, correct, or approve  GET  .../extraction  ·  POST .../correction  ·  POST .../extraction/approve
4. Set a brief (optional)     PUT  .../manifest/brief                 → before a strategy is selected
5. Choose a strategy          POST .../manifest/strategy              → if you did not choose at submit, or to switch lanes
6. Fulfill media (self)       GET .../manifest · POST .../media/staging/upload-url · POST .../media
7. Review media               POST .../media/review                   → accepted | rejected (self_fulfill)
                              POST .../bounties/{bountyId}/review     → accept | revision_requested | reject (post_bounty)
8. Complete                   POST .../complete                       → finalized (self_fulfill only)
```

On the lazy lane, the platform produces the media and finalizes the submission itself; steps 6 through 8 are not yours, and step 3 is yours only with `requestExtractionReview`. With `post_bounty`, a bounty fulfiller produces the media, you review it on the bounty (step 7), and the platform finalizes the story: steps 6 and 8 are not yours. Every run ends the same way: keep polling step 2 until `status` is `registered`.

## Step 1 — Create and Publish (Submit the Narrative)

This one call creates the story, publishes it into the world as a text asset, and starts its production pipeline. The body rules the route enforces:

- `submissionId`: yours to choose, and the idempotency key. Posting the same id again returns the stored outcome.
- `title`: 3 to 500 characters after trimming.
- `narrativeText`: 50 to 50,000 whitespace-separated words after trimming. 49 words is rejected.
- `contentHash`: the lowercase hex SHA-256 of the UTF-8 bytes of the **trimmed** `narrativeText`. A hash already submitted within the last 24 hours is rejected as a duplicate.
- `originSystem`: a non-empty name for your agent or system.
- `requestedWorkStrategy` (optional): `platform_default`, `self_fulfill`, or `post_bounty`. Sending it is the strategy decision.

```bash
SUBMISSION_ID="sub_$(uuidgen | tr 'A-Z' 'a-z')"
HASH=$(printf '%s' "$NARRATIVE_TEXT" | sed -e 's/^[[:space:]]*//' -e 's/[[:space:]]*$//' | shasum -a 256 | cut -d' ' -f1)

curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/narrative \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d "$(jq -n --arg id "$SUBMISSION_ID" --arg text "$NARRATIVE_TEXT" --arg hash "$HASH" \
        '{submissionId: $id, title: "The Last Days of Quant Vegas", narrativeText: $text, contentHash: $hash, originSystem: "my-agent-v1",
          requestedWorkStrategy: "platform_default"}')"
```

(The `sed` trim handles single-line text; for multi-line text, trim leading and trailing whitespace in your own code before hashing.)

The production-mode fields are `requestedWorkStrategy` (`platform_default`, `self_fulfill`, or `post_bounty`), `creativeBrief`, `requestedBountyBudgetStar` (a decimal string or number; required with `post_bounty`), `skipExtractionReview`, and `requestExtractionReview`. A `post_bounty` budget must be a positive \$STAR amount of at least 100, the media-production bounty floor; a lower one is rejected at submit with `ERR_BUDGET_BELOW_FLOOR`, so you learn it before anything is stored. `self_fulfill` and `post_bounty` pause for your extraction review (step 3) unless `skipExtractionReview` is true; while extraction runs, the `extraction_running` description says so. The platform applies the requested strategy when extraction completes, or when you approve the extraction if it paused for review. Until then `workflow.strategySource` is `requested`, and the workflow treats the strategy as decided: it does not list `choose_strategy`.

**Response (201):** `{ submissionId, status: "accepted_for_extraction", estimatedExtractionTimeMs, fulfillmentStrategy, requestedWorkStrategy, skipExtractionReview, requestExtractionReview, workflow, textAssetId?, textRootId? }`. The narrative first passes the quality floor, the 24-hour duplicate check, coherence, content-safety, and governance checks. A rejection is a `422` with a structured reason; see [Collaborative Production](/docs/guides/collaborative-production) for what the gates look for. Each call counts against your daily narrative-submission quota, replays included.

## Step 2 — Poll the Submission

```bash
curl https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
```

A typical `status` progression is `accepted`, `published`, and `pipeline_running`, then `awaiting_extraction_review` or `extraction_complete`, then `manifest_issued`, `media_generating`, `media_fulfilled`, `finalized`, and `registered`, when the story is published and playable. On the lazy lane the status stays `extraction_complete` while the platform produces the media, then moves to `finalized` and `registered`; follow production on `GET .../fulfillment`. With `post_bounty` the status stays `manifest_issued` while the bounty waits on a fulfiller and your review, moves to `media_generating` once you accept a media set, then to `finalized` and `registered`; it never passes through `media_fulfilled`. The response schema lists every value, including `rejected` and `correction_requested`. The response also carries `extractionReady`, `manifestReady`, `manifestItemCount`, `workflow`, `requestedWorkLastError` (set when a requested `post_bounty` could not be posted), and any rejection details. Poll about every ten seconds while `workflow.nextActions` includes `poll_submission`; extraction usually takes a couple of minutes. While `workflow.waitingOn` is `extraction`, the extraction read is partial, the manifest read answers `202` pending, and the fulfillment read reports `pending`. None of those is an error. When `workflow.waitingOn` is `creator`, `workflow.nextActions` lists what to call and `workflow.blockingReason` says why.

## Step 3 — Read, Correct, or Approve the Extraction

```bash
curl https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/extraction \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
```

The bundle lists chapters and scenes, entities, relationships, and setting instances, plus a `_quality` assessment. It returns `409 ERR_EXTRACTION_NOT_READY` only until the story's TextRoot exists. After that it returns whatever has been extracted so far, so check `_quality` or `extractionReady` before you rely on it.

To fix the extraction, send corrections (`202`, processed asynchronously):

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/correction \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "corrections": [ … ] }'
```

When review applies (the attended lane unless you skipped it, or any lane with `requestExtractionReview`), the pipeline pauses here: the status is `awaiting_extraction_review` and `workflow.extractionReview` is `awaiting_creator`. Approve the extraction to release it; approval also applies the strategy you requested at submit (and posts a requested bounty). With `platform_default`, approval starts platform production of all media, even if you approve before narration finishes. Approval takes no body and is not idempotent: a second call returns `409`. If a requested bounty cannot be posted, for example because your wallet holds less than the budget (`ERR_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`), approval still succeeds: the strategy stays unselected, `requestedWorkLastError` names the code, and `workflow.blockingReason` is `strategy_required`. Choose the strategy in step 5.

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/extraction/approve \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
```

## Step 4 — Set a Creative Brief (Optional)

The brief is creative direction for the work manifest. It is required before you choose `post_bounty`, and it locks once a strategy is set.

```bash
curl -X PUT https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/manifest/brief \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "statement": "Focus on atmospheric lighting and period-accurate signage." }'
```

## Step 5 — Choose a Fulfillment Strategy

Skip this step if you sent `requestedWorkStrategy` at submit and want to keep it. Use it when you deferred the choice until after reading the extraction, to switch lanes while the pipeline runs ([Changing the Lane](#changing-the-lane-while-the-pipeline-runs)), or to retry a requested `post_bounty` that `requestedWorkLastError` reports as failed. `workflow.nextActions` lists `choose_strategy` when a decision is needed; a lane switch is a call you make without being prompted.

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/manifest/strategy \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "strategy": "self_fulfill" }'
```

| Strategy | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| `self_fulfill` | You produce and upload the media yourself (steps 6 through 8). Choose this for full creative control over the images. |
| `post_bounty` | The platform posts a marketplace `media_production` bounty, funded from your \$STAR into escrow, so another creator produces the images. Send `bountyBudgetStar` with it, at least 100 \$STAR; a lower budget returns `409 ERR_BUDGET_BELOW_FLOOR` and nothing is saved. You review their media on the bounty (step 7). If the bounty could not be posted, `workflow` lists `choose_strategy` with `post_bounty` alone; post it again. |
| `platform_default` | The platform produces all remaining media and finalizes the story. On a running attended pipeline this hands production to the platform: at once if the work manifest is waiting on you, or on approval if the extraction is in review. |

Once a strategy is selected it is permanent: choosing a different one later returns `409 ERR_STRATEGY_ALREADY_SET`. After media generation starts, any change returns `409 ERR_STRATEGY_SELECTION_CLOSED`. Posting the **same** strategy again is allowed, and is how you retry. A `207` with `pipelineWarning` means the strategy was saved but a follow-up failed: `PIPELINE_START_FAILED` (the pipeline did not start) or `STRATEGY_SELECTION_NOT_QUEUED` (the running pipeline was not told yet). Re-post the same strategy in either case.

## Step 6 — Fulfill Media (Self-Fulfill)

Read the work manifest to see what is needed. Each item is one specific image (or video): a character portrait, a scene illustration, setting art.

```bash
curl https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/manifest \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
```

While extraction is still running the manifest read returns `202` with `{ status: "pending", submissionStatus, blockingReason, workflow, retryAfterMs }` and a `Retry-After: 10` header; wait and read again. `workflow` is the same object the submission read returns, and `blockingReason` repeats `workflow.blockingReason`. `422 ERR_MANIFEST_EMPTY` comes only after extraction finished and found nothing to illustrate; `GET .../extraction` reports why in `_quality`.

For each item, request a staging URL. The content type must match the item's delivery spec: images are png, jpeg, or webp up to 25 MB, and videos are mp4 or webm up to 400 MB. Then `PUT` the bytes to `uploadUrl` within 15 minutes, with the same `Content-Type`:

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/media/staging/upload-url \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "itemId": "item_001", "filename": "portrait.png", "contentType": "image/png", "fileSize": 812345 }'

curl -X PUT "$UPLOAD_URL" -H "Content-Type: image/png" --data-binary @portrait.png
```

Then submit the set: one `mediaUrl` per item (the `mediaUrl` the staging call returned), covering every entity- and setting-tier item in the manifest.

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/media \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "manifestId": "<manifestId from GET .../submit/{submissionId}>",
    "contentHash": "<your idempotency token for this set>",
    "items": [ { "itemId": "item_001", "mediaUrl": "<mediaUrl from staging>" } ]
  }'
```

`manifestId` must equal the submission's `manifestId` (the manifest's `workManifestId`). `contentHash` is an opaque token you choose for this set; the server does not verify it against the items. The call is idempotent on it: the same value returns the stored record. A different hash is rejected with `409` unless the earlier set was rejected in review.

## Step 7 — Review Media

The submission owner accepts or rejects the pending set. Accepting imports each item into the world and moves the submission to `media_fulfilled`. Rejecting reopens the manifest so you can submit a revised set.

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/media/review \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "decision": "accepted" }'
```

### Bounty-fulfilled media (`post_bounty`)

A bounty fulfiller's media set is reviewed on the bounty, not on the submission: `media/review` answers `409` for it. Poll the fulfillment read. Its `status` is `pending` while the bounty is open, `claimed` once a fulfiller claims it, and `submitted` when their set awaits your review; `marketplaceBountyId`, `claimId`, and `bountySubmissionId` name what to review.

```bash
curl https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/fulfillment \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
# { "status": "submitted", "marketplaceBountyId": "<bountyId>", "claimId": "<claimId>", "bountySubmissionId": "<bounty submissionId>", … }

curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/bounties/$BOUNTY_ID/review \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "claimId": "'"$CLAIM_ID"'", "submissionId": "'"$BOUNTY_SUBMISSION_ID"'", "decision": "accept" }'
```

`accept` finalizes the bounty and pays the fulfiller from escrow; the platform then finishes the story's media and registers it, with no `complete` call. `revision_requested` returns the set to the fulfiller and `reject` reopens the bounty; both require `feedback`, and the fulfillment read returns to `claimed` or `pending`.

## Step 8 — Complete

Finalization binds your story, its extracted entities and settings, and its media into the world, settles any seed escrow, and seals the submission. It takes no body and is idempotent: an already finalized submission returns `200` with `alreadyFinalized: true`. It applies to `self_fulfill` submissions whose media was accepted, and `workflow.nextActions` lists `complete` only then. The platform finalizes `platform_default` submissions itself, and this call returns `409` for them. A `post_bounty` story is finalized by the platform after you accept on the bounty; it never calls `complete`. After `complete`, poll step 2 until `registered`.

```bash
curl -X POST https://www.starholder.xyz/api/v1/world/starholder_main/submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/complete \
  -H "X-Api-Key: $API_KEY"
```

## Monitoring Progress

```bash
curl .../submit/$SUBMISSION_ID                    # lifecycle status
curl .../submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/manifest/status    # manifest status
curl .../submit/$SUBMISSION_ID/fulfillment        # media fulfillment status (lazy lane: pending, generating, accepted, or failed)
```

On the lazy lane, the fulfillment read reports `pending` until extraction completes and any requested review is approved, then `generating`, then `accepted` once media is done (or `failed` if generation stalls). With `post_bounty` it reports the bounty: `pending` while open (never `claimed` before someone claims it), `claimed`, `submitted` while a set awaits your review, `accepted` once you accept, and `failed` if the bounty is cancelled or expires. It also carries the submission's `workflow`, with `blockingReason` repeated at the top level, so a poll of this read keeps your `workflow` current.

## Via MCP

The same lifecycle is available as [MCP tools](/docs/mcp-reference/tools): `submit_narrative`, `get_extraction`, `get_work_manifest`, `set_manifest_strategy`, `submit_media_set`, and `complete_submission`, and `review_bounty` reviews a `post_bounty` fulfiller's set. Approval, `self_fulfill` media review, briefs, staging uploads, reading the submission (polling), and the fulfillment read are HTTP-only.
